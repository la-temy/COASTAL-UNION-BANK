# Coastal Union Bank – Crisis Communication Plan & Tabletop Exercise

## Project Overview
Developed a crisis communication plan and tabletop exercise for Coastal Union Bank, focusing on a major core banking system outage affecting 40 branches, payment processing and digital banking services.

## Key Findings
- Business continuity and disaster recovery arrangements have not been formally tested.
- A major outage could disrupt customer access to funds, transactions and essential banking services.
- Unclear escalation and recovery arrangements could delay crisis decisions.
- Third-party payment processor dependencies increase operational risk.
- Poorly coordinated communication could damage customer confidence and create regulatory concerns.

## Key Recommendations
- Establish clear crisis declaration and escalation authority.
- Activate a Crisis Management Team (CMT) with defined responsibilities.
- Assign an authorised Communications Lead for customer, employee and media updates.
- Coordinate technical recovery, branch workarounds and third-party engagement.
- Assess regulatory and contractual notification obligations.
- Maintain incident timelines, decision logs and communication records.

## Tabletop Exercise
**Scenario:** Core banking system outage.

**Duration:** 60–90 minutes.

The exercise uses three stages:
1. Initial system outage and crisis escalation.
2. Payment processing failure and transaction reconciliation.
3. Customer complaints, media enquiries and regulatory concerns.

## Key Gap Analysis

| Identified Gap | Recommended Improvement |
|---|---|
| Untested business continuity and disaster recovery arrangements | Conduct regular exercises and validate recovery objectives. |
| Potentially unclear crisis activation arrangements | Define declaration authority and escalation thresholds. |
| Third-party payment dependency | Assign relationship owners and formalise recovery coordination. |
| Customer and media communication risks | Establish approved communication channels, messages and spokesperson authority. |
| Unstructured incident records and follow-up | Maintain decision logs, action trackers and lessons-learned reports. |

## Expected Deliverables
- Crisis Communication Plan.
- Crisis Management Team roles and responsibilities.
- Customer communication message.
- Core banking outage tabletop exercise.
- Scenario injects, discussion questions and evaluation criteria.
- Post-exercise findings and corrective action tracker.

## Skills Demonstrated
Crisis management, business continuity, disaster recovery, stakeholder communication, third-party risk coordination and GRC exercise design.
